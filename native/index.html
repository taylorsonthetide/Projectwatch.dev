About your comparison

Wave height is significant wave height, not the largest individual wave. Period is the mean interval between waves. Wind is modelled at 10 m above the surface.

Green means the available forecast is within your settings. Amber means conditions approach a limit or waves have a short period. Red means a limit is exceeded, or near-limit waves combine with a short period. Grey means data is missing, stale or incomplete.

These are your comfort preferences, not a measure of your boat’s capability. Check current local conditions, official warnings, tide and harbour advice before deciding to go.
