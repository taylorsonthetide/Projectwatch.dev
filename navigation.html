    Your vessel

    Draught is the depth of your keel below the waterline. The warning threshold is your chosen minimum total water depth, not clearance below the keel.

    Stored in this browser on this device, separate from your training account.

    Depth alerts unavailable. Settings are ready for future licensed charts. No reliable depth or chart-datum tide data is connected, so GPS cannot trigger shallow-water warnings yet.

    Trip recorder & logbook

    Timed entries align to clock times. Actual GPS fix times are shown separately. The travelled track is recorded more frequently.

    Use GPS or Run demo in Controls, then start a trip.

    Keep navigation visible. Recording stops and saves when you leave the page, change position source or move the app into the background. GPS gaps are never filled with invented positions. Times and ETA use GPS speed; no weather or tide correction.
