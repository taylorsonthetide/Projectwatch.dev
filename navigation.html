    Your vessel

    Personal comfort settings, not a vessel capability limit. Short periods count when waves reach half your height limit or 1 m, whichever is lower. Height at 80% of your limit is amber; exceeding it, or combining near-limit height with short periods, is red. Tiny short-period ripples do not trigger a rough-trip warning.

    About the N43 starter and wave heights

    There is no established maximum comfort height or period for every Nordhavn 43 owner. The 2 m / 9 s starter is a personal simulation example. Heading, speed, stabilisers and local conditions matter.

    Forecast height is significant wave height (Hs), the average of the highest third of waves. Individual waves may occasionally reach around twice Hs: a 2 m forecast can include waves around 4 m. This is not a predicted maximum.

    The period setting compares the mean period of the combined sea, not the peak period of the swell.

    N43 owner experience: Kosmos · Met Office: wave heights

    Draught is the depth of your keel below the waterline. The warning threshold is your chosen minimum total water depth, not clearance below the keel.
