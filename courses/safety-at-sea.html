ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

A safe trip starts before the lines come off. This pathway explores preparation, hazards, crew safety and responses to changing conditions through six visual lessons designed for recreational boaters.

  • Preparing for departure
  • Spotting hazards
  • Moving safely on deck
  • Checking safety equipment
  • People and changing conditions
  • Emergency response

Put the learning into practice

Build a preparation routine and recognise hazards affecting the boat and the people aboard.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
