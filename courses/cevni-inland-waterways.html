ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

Inland boating has its own rules, signs and signals. This pathway introduces CEVNI through nine modules, helping you recognise waterway markings and vessel signals and work through practical traffic situations before a separate mock assessment.

  • Inland rules
  • Signs and marks
  • Vessel lights and day signals
  • Sound signals
  • Traffic situations
  • Locks and structures
  • Mock assessment

Put the learning into practice

Build recognition of inland signs and vessel signals, then apply that knowledge to locks, structures and traffic situations.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
