ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

Knowing the basics of a marine diesel helps you understand routine checks and recognise when something needs attention. This pathway uses visual lessons to connect the engine cycle with fuel, cooling, lubrication, servicing and sensible fault finding.

  • Four-stroke cycle
  • Checks and servicing
  • Fuel and filters
  • Diesel bug
  • Raw-water cooling
  • Impeller care
  • Lubrication
  • Fault finding

Put the learning into practice

Understand what routine checks are looking for and how the fuel and cooling systems support the engine.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
