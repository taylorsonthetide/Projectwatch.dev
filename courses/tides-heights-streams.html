ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

Tides affect both the water available beneath your boat and the movement of water along your passage. This pathway connects tidal heights with tidal streams so you can practise thinking about depth, timing, set and drift together.

  • Tide cycle
  • Chart datum
  • Tide tables
  • Tidal heights
  • Tidal streams
  • Set and drift
  • Stream atlas
  • Passage windows

Put the learning into practice

Practise interpreting tide information and connecting stream direction and strength with passage timing.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
