ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

A passage plan brings the boat, crew, route and conditions together. This pathway follows preparation through to monitoring the journey, keeping useful logbook entries and considering alternatives when the original plan needs to change.

  • Appraisal
  • Route planning
  • Depth and timing
  • Fuel and alternatives
  • Crew briefing
  • Monitoring the passage
  • Logbook entries

Put the learning into practice

Work through appraisal, route planning, depth and timing, fuel, alternatives and monitoring.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
