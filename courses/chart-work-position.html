ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

A chart connects your position with the water around you. This pathway introduces chart reading and position plotting, then explores visual fixes, dead reckoning, estimated positions and ways to cross-check GPS information.

  • Reading a chart
  • Latitude and longitude
  • Distance and direction
  • Visual fixes
  • Dead reckoning
  • Estimated position
  • GPS cross-checks

Put the learning into practice

Practise plotting positions and understand how different position-finding methods can be checked against each other.

Work at your own pace and revisit the subjects you want to practise. Your free Helmlore account keeps your learning progress together.
