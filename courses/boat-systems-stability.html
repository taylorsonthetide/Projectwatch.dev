ABOUT THIS PATHWAY

What you’ll explore

Understanding your boat makes preparation and everyday checks more meaningful. This pathway looks at loading and stability alongside the systems that keep a recreational vessel afloat, powered and under control.

  • Loading and stability
  • Watertight integrity
  • Propulsion
  • Fuel systems
  • Electrical power
  • Steering
  • Responding to problems
