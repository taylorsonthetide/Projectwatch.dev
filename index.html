A few things to know.

Is Helmlore free?

Yes. The training courses are free to access with a Helmlore account.

Does Helmlore award an official boating qualification?

Helmlore provides independent recreational learning and practice. It does not award an RYA or MCA qualification or replace recognised practical training.

Can I use Helmlore on my iPad?

You can open the training in your web browser on an iPad, phone or computer. Sign in to continue with your saved learning progress.

How can I contact Helmlore?

Email Helmlore.com@gmail.com with questions or feedback.
