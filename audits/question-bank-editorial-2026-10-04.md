# Helmlore question-bank corrections — 4 October 2026

Approved editorial follow-up to the complete-library audit. Changes are confined to training content, feedback, sign distractor selection, asset version queries and regression checks. Historical banks remain historical; current banks and their stored canonical representations are corrected. This is independent learning material, not examiner approval or a certificate of regulatory compliance.

## Primary-source basis and amendment check

* COLREGs Rules 11, 19(e), 23(a)(ii), 25(e), 26(c)(ii), 30 and 35: MCA published COLREG text and current MSN 1781 Amendment 3; restricted-visibility context also checked against MCA MGN 369 Amendment 1. International provisions are used, not US Inland alternatives.
* IALA R1001 Maritime Buoyage System, Region A: lateral direction, preferred channels, cardinal marks, isolated danger, safe water, special marks and emergency wreck marking. Existing correctly keyed visual models and light selections are retained.
* CEVNI Revision 6, Corrigenda 1/2: Article 4.07 small-craft exception corroborated by the indexed official corrected publication; Annex 8 downstream reference and Article 1.18 fairway obstruction are the bases for the clarified items.
* **Amendment 1**, ECE/TRANS/SC.3/115/Rev.6/Amend.1 (2025): Articles 1.02, 1.03, 1.16 and 8.02 pronouns; Article 1.11(3) accepts electronic versions of the documents in paragraphs 1/2 when consultable electronically at any time; Article 3.07 title changes; Article 10.01(2)(d) updates the definition of other special waste. These do not alter the keyed module 8 AIS exception or reporting questions corrected here. An electronic document does not remove the underlying document obligation.
* **Amendment 2**, ECE/TRANS/SC.3/115/Rev.6/Amend.2 (2025): Article 1.03(4) extends crew-fitness wording to impairment for any other reason; Article 3.02 requires continuous, uniform light, Annex 4 dispersion/colour and Annex 5 intensity, with the specified stationary non-motorised-vessel night-mark exception and visibility of at least 1,000 metres under the stated conditions. It does not amend Article 1.02 boatmaster fatigue wording. No existing assessed item on these amended technical provisions is changed by inference.

Official amendment texts: https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=ECE%2FTRANS%2FSC.3%2F115%2FRev.6%2FAmend.1&t=pdf and https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=ECE%2FTRANS%2FSC.3%2F115%2FRev.6%2FAmend.2&t=pdf.

## Authorship and validation

Revised prose was written specifically for Helmlore in this approved editorial pass, from the assessed proposition and the primary rule basis above. It was not copied from a commercial question bank. That statement applies to the revised prose, not to the provenance of every historic record.

Validation compares every modified bank with its pre-change version: question counts, option counts, answer indexes, IDs, source references, visuals, categories, critical flags, pass marks and other non-editorial metadata are preserved. Current CEVNI JS and JSON mirrors agree. Tests also cover distinct sign meanings, AIS feedback, and the existing chartwork, passage and diesel checks. Authentication, accounts, owner permissions and navigation software are outside this change.
