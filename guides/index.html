Boating questions: free beginner guides

New to boating, returning after a break or refreshing a subject? Start with a question. These free guides explain the basics and show you where to practise next.

  • I want to buy a boat. Where do I start?A complete UK buying guide, from budget and paperwork to surveys, engine checks and handover.
  • What do red and green buoys mean?Learn Region A lateral marks with our free visual sample.
  • How do cardinal marks work?Learn north, east, south and west cardinal marks: compass directions, black and yellow colours, cone topmarks and white light rhythms.
  • Who gives way when two boats cross?Understand crossing situations between power-driven vessels, give-way and stand-on duties, and why everyone must keep assessing collision risk.
  • What do boat navigation lights mean?A beginner guide to red and green sidelights, masthead and stern lights, viewing direction and boat navigation-light recognition.
  • What should I learn before starting boating?Start learning boating basics free: safety, buoyage, lights and shapes, collision regulations, tides and navigation before practical training.
  • What is CEVNI and what is the test for?Understand CEVNI inland waterway rules, the ICC inland category and how free Helmlore practice differs from an official CEVNI test.
  • Boat pre-departure checklist UKA practical pre-departure guide covering weather, tides, crew, lifejackets, communications, boat checks and a plan for returning.
