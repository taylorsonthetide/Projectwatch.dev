What do boat navigation lights mean?

Navigation lights help you recognise a vessel's aspect and status at night. Red is the port sidelight, green is starboard, and white lights have different meanings depending on their position and the full arrangement.

Why does green appear on your left?

When looking directly towards another vessel's bow, its starboard side is on your left. Its red port light appears on your right. Do not swap the vessel's port and starboard to match your own viewpoint.

What can a white light mean?

A single white light is not enough to identify every vessel. It could be a sternlight, an anchor light or part of another permitted arrangement. Look for the complete pattern, movement and viewing angle; use all available information.

Is a sailing boat always showing sailing lights?

A boat using its engine for propulsion is power-driven even if its sails remain up. The applicable light arrangement also depends on size and circumstances. Learn the relevant exceptions rather than assuming that every boat shows an identical pattern.

What should I learn next?

Study lights together with day shapes, vessel status and the collision rules. Recognition helps you understand what you see; it does not replace a lookout or an assessment of collision risk.
