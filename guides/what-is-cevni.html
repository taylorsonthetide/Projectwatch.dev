What is CEVNI and what is the test for?

CEVNI is the European Code for Inland Waterways. It covers inland navigation rules, including signs, signals, vessel markings and conduct on waterways. It is a different learning subject from coastal COLREGs.

How does CEVNI relate to the ICC?

For a UK ICC issued by the RYA, applicants needing the inland category validated must pass the RYA ICC CEVNI test. Passing that test demonstrates the required knowledge of CEVNI; it does not, on its own, replace the other eligibility and competence requirements for an ICC.

Do I need it for every boating trip?

The answer depends on the country, waters, vessel and documents required. Do not assume the same requirements apply everywhere. Check current destination rules and the guidance from the certificate issuer before booking a trip.

What should I study?

  • Waterway signs and marks.
  • Vessel lights and day displays.
  • Sound signals and communications.
  • Meeting, crossing, overtaking and other traffic situations.
  • Locks, bridges and local navigation instructions.

Can Helmlore issue my test certificate?

No. Helmlore's CEVNI pathway provides nine learning modules and a mock assessment for practice. It is not the official RYA ICC CEVNI test and does not issue an ICC or official test certificate. Use the RYA's current guidance to find the appropriate official test route.

Where do I begin?

Explore signs and marks, then work through the CEVNI modules in order. Keep inland displays distinct from coastal rules where they differ.
