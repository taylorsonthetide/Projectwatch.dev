What should I check before leaving a marina?

Before departure, check the conditions and your plan, brief the crew, prepare safety and communication equipment, and complete the checks appropriate to your boat. Allow time to deal with a problem before releasing the lines.

Conditions and the passage

Review the marine weather, visibility, sea conditions and tidal information for the whole trip, including the return. Check charted hazards, harbour instructions, entrance restrictions and alternatives if conditions change.

Crew and communications

  • Choose and wear suitable lifejackets or buoyancy aids for the activity.
  • Brief everyone on movement around the boat and what to do in an emergency.
  • Have an appropriate, working way to call for help, accessible when needed.
  • Tell a contact ashore your plan and when to expect you back.

The boat itself

Use your vessel's checklist and equipment manuals. Check propulsion, steering, fuel, batteries and essential systems. Look for leaks, secure loose equipment and prepare lines and fenders for the manoeuvre. Use the kill cord correctly on powered craft fitted with one.

What if something is wrong?

Resolve it, change the plan or delay departure. A checklist helps you remember tasks; it cannot decide whether the conditions or equipment are suitable for your vessel and crew.
