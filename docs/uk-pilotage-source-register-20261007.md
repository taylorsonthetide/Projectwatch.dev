# Helmlore — UK and Ireland pilotage source register

Research date: 7 October 2026. A coast-by-coast discovery register, not a claim that every UK harbour or every published pilot has been audited. Sources are publisher, author, club, harbour-authority or government pages. Paid-book contents have not been acquired or reviewed in full. Listing a source does not mean its data is licensed for incorporation into Helmlore.

## Findings for the map

Pilot books can identify named channels, leading marks, perches, shoals, entry constraints, anchorages, reported depths, locks and harbour contacts. They generally do not supply a complete, maintained GIS inventory of marks or a bathymetric surface. A stated dredged depth, a least depth, a depth alongside and a tidal height are different quantities and must remain separate.

The strongest new free book located is NGA Publication 142: Ireland and the West Coast of England, 17th edition (2024), 204 PDF pages. Its preface states updates through 26 March 2026. It covers the Irish coast and England/Wales west-coast approaches, including a Ribble description in sector 10, paragraph 10.2. That paragraph does not provide all Preston perches. The preface directs readers to light lists for full light information; the pilot is not a substitute for a light database.

Preston Marina explicitly offers John Eccles's Ribble Pilot, created in 2010 and uploaded January 2018. It is free to download but the author retains copyright and the marina disclaims ongoing accuracy. Treat its marks as historical candidates and reconcile each against current council/marina notices. Do not plot them as current without verification.

Milford Haven is particularly promising: its authority resource library lists a 2026 leisure guide, entry/departure guidance, directions, and four tidal-stream atlas volumes. These are local references, not evidence of permission to republish their maps.

The other three NGA regional PDFs were also retrieved and their title/preface pages checked. Together the four volumes contain 1,206 PDF pages; this is regional commercial-coastal coverage, not every leisure harbour. Their cover states no copyright claimed under Title 17 U.S.C.; check individual credited third-party material and UK reuse implications before publishing extracts.

## Amendments worth tracking

- [Imray corrections and book supplements](https://www.imray.com/about/updates/): free supplements for many current books. Also lists 2026 River Deben/Woodbridge Haven and River Ore/Orford Haven chartlets based on Trinity House surveys.
- [Clyde Cruising Club amendments](https://clydecruisingclub.org/page/sailing-directions): yearly corrections can include changed light sequences, anchorages and newly reported rocks.
- [East Coast Pilot author site](https://www.eastcoastpilot.com/): author updates, plus North Sea Passage Pilot updates.
- [Fernhurst series](https://fernhurstbooks.com/books/series/cruising_companions/): book-specific updates; inspect each edition's resources.
- [Preston Marina notices](https://prestonmarina.co.uk/index.php/notices-to-mariners-preston-dock-river-ribble/): mixed historical/current notices; do not overlook cancellations.
- [UKHO weekly notices](https://www.admiralty.co.uk/publications/publications-and-reference-guides/admiralty-sailing-directions): updates to official pilots.

## What to record before adding any feature

For each candidate: feature name/type, harbour, exact source URL, book edition, page/paragraph, notice number, publication/update date, retrieval date, coordinate precision and stated datum, current/inactive/historical status, units and vertical datum for depths, licence/permission and independent corroboration. Retain conflicting records rather than silently choosing one. Coarse coordinates in regional prose must not become precise harbour marks.

Useful additions could be a harbour-information layer and source-linked pilotage cards. Only verified, appropriately reusable spatial facts should become live chart features. Paid books and free PDFs remain research/reference sources unless specific rights allow republishing. A bought book does not supply an app-data licence.
