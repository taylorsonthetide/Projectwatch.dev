# Chart Work & Position — 1.96.0

Independent recreational training added to the tested 1.95.7 baseline. Existing pathway scripts, content and assets are not edited. The chart pathway is loaded from an external content library, engine, stylesheet and original chart assets.

## Teaching progression

Four beginner steps introduce chart purpose, position coordinates, distance/time and the distinction between fix, DR and EP. Twelve detailed lessons cover chart scale/units/datums/reliability; latitude/longitude and formats; distance and time; true course and compass references; two-bearing fixes; third lines, cocked hats, transits and range checks; DR; EP/current/leeway; course to steer; GPS/GNSS and independent checks; pilotage; log records and fallback navigation. There are four guided practices, an editable DR/EP chart workshop and 21 original knowledge-check questions.

## Artwork and numerical basis

PW-CW01 Alder Bay is a fictional coastal training area. There is no real-world survey, chart licence or navigational validity. Coastline, depths, names, marks and tide numbers are invented and repeatedly labelled as training data. The nautical chart styling is original vector artwork generated by `tools/build-chartwork-charts.py`; no UKHO, RYA, commercial or NOAA chart is traced or reproduced. IALA Region A is used for the fictional harbour entry.

The chart uses a calculated spherical Mercator projection between 50°00′–50°10′ N and 004°20′–004°00′ W. Graticule, minor latitude ticks, true compass ticks and plotted points are calculated. Distance uses the near-local latitude scale. Eleven plates show the same area with separate teaching overlays; illustrations remain in `assets/chartwork/`. Metadata records bounds and plotting coordinates. The interactive overlay uses the same projection and bounds.

The DR/EP workshop is a short-leg rhumb-line model, using the conventional approximation of 60 NM per latitude degree, zero leeway and constant direction/rate. EP adds an estimated current displacement over the same time as the through-water run. Out-of-chart results are clearly reported. The worked eastward run is 3 NM at 6 kn for 30 minutes; a 2 kn northward stream adds 1 NM north. The course-to-steer example independently balances 2 kn north with 2 kn south from a 6 kn vessel vector: 109.471° T and 5.657 kn eastward over ground. It is not a passage plan.

## Verification

`node tests/chartwork-math.cjs` checks Mercator forward/inverse round trips, cardinal runs, DR/EP calculations, coordinate formatting and minute rollover, an independent course-to-steer vector, metadata agreement, unique lesson IDs, question structure and all lesson chart assets. SVG plates are rendered and inspected visually. Browser interaction checks are recorded separately when complete.
