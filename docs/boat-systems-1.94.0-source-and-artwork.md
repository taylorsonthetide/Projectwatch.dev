# Boat Systems & Stability — 1.94.0

Independent recreational learning module. Eight illustrated lessons, four decision practice cards, and twelve original self-check questions. This is a principles course, not a vessel-specific stability assessment, engineering instruction or RYA/MCA qualification.

Content basis: RYA guidance on preparing a boat for the season (seacocks, hoses, pumps and electrical checks), diesel engine care, fuel contamination, electrical maintenance, free water and loading, and pleasure-vessel equipment. The links are stored in `libraries/project-watch/boat-systems-1.94.0.js` and shown in the module. The RYA checklist for training craft was consulted for breadth but its commercial/centre obligations are not presented as universal private-boat rules. Manufacturers' manuals govern the learner's actual boat.

All eight schematic SVGs in `assets/boat-systems/` are original code-native Project Watch teaching assets. They are referenced by file path from the external lesson library. No image data or questions are embedded in `index.html`. The diagrams deliberately omit safe heel angles, numerical capacity limits, GM, GZ curves and stability calculators because these cannot be inferred generically for a recreational boat. Workshop responses are illustrative decisions only.

Separate progress key: `pw-boat-systems-reviewed-v1`. Previous Project Watch and CEVNI pathways remain independent.
