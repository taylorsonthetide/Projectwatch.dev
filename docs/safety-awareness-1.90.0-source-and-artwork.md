# Safety Awareness at Sea — source and artwork register

Build 1.91.0, reviewed 26 September 2026. Independent original practice for recreational boaters. It is not an RYA or MCA qualification, and practical training remains separate.

## Guidance used

| Source | Teaching use |
| --- | --- |
| [RYA passage planning](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/passage-planning-and-navigation/passage-planning/) | Conditions, crew briefing, contingency and shore contact. |
| [RYA water safety](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/) | Safety equipment and crew preparation. |
| [RYA equipment for UK pleasure vessels](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/equipment-for-uk-pleasure-vessels/) | Recreational equipment context; no commercial carriage rule is applied to pleasure craft. |
| [RYA safe speed and proper lookout](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/safe-speed-and-proper-look-out/) | Lookout and changing conditions. |
| [RYA Basic Sea Survival course](https://www.rya.org.uk/course-finder/basic-sea-survival-certificate/) | Separate practical training boundary. |
| [MCA MGN 592 (M+F) Amendment 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mgn-592-mf-anchoring-mooring-towing-or-hauling-equipment/mgn-592-mf-amendment-2-anchoring-mooring-towing-or-hauling-equipment-on-all-vessels), sections 4–5 | Source for loaded mooring line, bight and snap-back guidance. This guidance addresses vessel operations broadly; its commercial requirements are not presented as pleasure-craft legal duties. Section 5.2 warns that the danger is not limited to the straight line of pull and that the wider deck can be hazardous. |

The former question 5 answer, “Away from the line of pull and moving gear,” was too imprecise to identify a safe place. It was rewritten as a decision to stop hauling, keep others clear, assess line lead, bights and possible snap-back, then ease the load safely. No single standing position is represented as universally safe.

Seafish fishing training and MCA fishing-specific requirements informed early scoping but are outside the learner-facing recreational course. No source text, assessment questions, course photos or logos are copied.
