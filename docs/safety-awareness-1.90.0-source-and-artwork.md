# Safety Awareness at Sea — source and artwork register

Build 1.90.0. This independent Project Watch course is for general recreational seamanship awareness, with clearly identified commercial fishing context. It is not a Seafish, MCA or RYA qualification and cannot substitute for required practical training or certification.

## Source basis (reviewed 26 September 2026)

| Source | Role in original teaching |
| --- | --- |
| [Seafish commercial fishing training](https://www.seafish.org/safety-and-training/seagoing-training-and-certificates/commercial-fishing-training-courses/) | Clarifies that Safety Awareness is a separate one-day mandatory basic safety subject for experienced fishers. The under-16.5 m skipper certificate and stability courses are distinct. |
| [MCA MIN 722](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/min-722-mf-clarification-of-the-basic-health-and-safety-and-safety-awareness-training-requirements-for-fishers/min-722-mf-clarification-of-the-basic-health-and-safety-and-safety-awareness-training-requirements-for-fishers) | Current prerequisite and experience clarification for the Seafish course. Avoids presenting this module as equivalent. |
| [MCA fishing vessel risk assessment](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/fishing-vessel-risk-assessments-and-safety-management-systems) | Hazard identification, crew involvement, controls and review when operations change. Commercial legal duties are identified as specific to fishing vessels. |
| [MCA fishing health and safety](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/fishing-vessel-health-and-safety) | Deck risks, fatigue, equipment checks and protective equipment. |
| [MCA PFD guidance MGN 588 Amendment 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mgn-588-f-amendment-2-compulsory-provision-and-wearing-of-personal-flotation-devices-on-fishing-vessels/mgn-588-f-amendment-2-compulsory-provision-and-wearing-of-personal-flotation-devices-on-fishing-vessels) | Fishing-specific PFD context; not stated as a universal pleasure-vessel rule. |
| [RYA passage planning](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/passage-planning-and-navigation/passage-planning/) | Pre-departure briefing, crew familiarity, contingency plans. |
| [RYA water safety](https://www.rya.org.uk/water-safety/) and [Basic Sea Survival overview](https://www.rya.org.uk/course-finder/basic-sea-survival-certificate/) | Scope boundary: personal flotation, emergency equipment and sea survival are related but practical training is separate. |

No source text, course slides, exam questions, photos or logos are copied. The course uses short original explanations and Project Watch scenarios. Facts and training guidance should be reviewed when official sources change.
