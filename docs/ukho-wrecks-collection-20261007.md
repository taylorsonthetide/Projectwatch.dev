Collected all IDs returned at retrieval: 25,890 points and 161 area records. Queries batched in groups of 500 to respect record limits. Each batch count checked against requested IDs; final feature IDs checked for uniqueness. Output EPSG:4326 longitude/latitude, all properties retained. Changes: exported as GeoJSON in EPSG:4326; no manual geometry or field edits. Counts are current service records, not necessarily unique wrecks or current navigation hazards. Area and point records may overlap. Source updates can change these counts.

Coverage is the source UK EEZ dataset; it is not national Irish coverage. Records include wrecks and obstructions, live/dead or historical data. This public export exposes only fid, objnam, globalid (plus area length/area fields); no classification, hazard status, depth, positional uncertainty or survey dates were supplied. Those details require a richer source before interpretation. Retrieval date is not survey date. This is research data, not a complete certified navigation chart. Not yet added to the live map.
