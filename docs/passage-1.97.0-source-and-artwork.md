# Passage Planning & Logbook — 1.97.0

Original recreational teaching: three beginner steps, ten lessons, fifteen self-check questions, planning notes and practice navigation log. Reviewed 29 September 2026. This is independent learning, not an accredited qualification or statutory logbook.

Guidance informs original explanations and questions. No copied course text, commercial chart images, RNLI photographs or third-party artwork. Course content in libraries/project-watch; graphics in assets/passage; UI in js/passage and css/passage. Original illustrated plates generated with tools/build-passage-visuals.py. The route plate reuses Project Watch's fictional Alder Bay Mercator chart. Direct-line example crosses East Island; planned detour avoids it. It is not a real route, and depth contours/geography are invented. Timing and fuel examples are separate hypothetical exercises and not computed from the chart route.

Worked numbers: 6/5 h = 72 min; 4/5 h = 48 min; plus 30 min = 150 min. Static clearance 0.8 + 2.0 − 1.6 = 1.2 m, before other allowances. Fuel 2.5×8 + 1×8 + 10 = 38 L; reserve chosen only for illustration, not a prescribed rule.

Planner and log use separate browser localStorage keys. No account, server sync, automatic route validation or shore-contact message sending. Plain-text plan and CSV log exports are local downloads. Log text is HTML-escaped, CSV formula prefixes neutralised. Required entry fields: date/time/basis/position/source/observation or action. Export backups: browser storage may be cleared or unavailable. Worksheet completion is not passage approval.
