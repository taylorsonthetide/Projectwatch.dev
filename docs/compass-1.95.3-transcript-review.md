## Technical distinctions retained

- A heading is bow direction; it does not by itself establish course through water or over ground.
- GPS COG is a direction of motion over ground while moving; it may be unstable at low speed and is not automatically heading.
- A bearing to one charted object gives one line of position, not a complete fix. A 045° T bearing from boat to object corresponds to the reciprocal 225° T line back from the object.
- Variation differs by location/date; deviation is boat and heading specific. Real values require current chart and checked boat-specific information.
- Boat-specific compass adjustment is left to competent marine practice. Fictional scenes are not navigation instructions.
