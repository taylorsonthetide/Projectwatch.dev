What these prices include

  • Annual berth tariff, VAT where confirmed, and separately published compulsory fees where their amount and basis are clear.
  • Boat-length or berth-size rules, minimum lengths and explicit tariff bands. A gap or overlap in the published bands produces “ask marina”, rather than a guessed rate.
  • Metered electricity, optional services, refundable deposits, travel, maintenance and inland navigation licences are excluded unless explicitly identified. This is a berth-price guide, not a full ownership budget.
  • Rates paid upfront can differ from monthly contracts. Promotions, payment deadlines, restricted berths, membership and basin-access conditions appear in the tariff notes.
  • GBP and EUR are kept separate. No currency conversion or claim of a single UK/Ireland cheapest price is made.

Where is the cheapest marina for my boat?

The lowest price depends on your boat length, location, berth type and the published tariffs available. This calculator ranks eligible researched prices in your selected currency. It cannot guarantee the cheapest available berth nationwide.

Are these swinging mooring prices?

No. This guide compares physical marina and pontoon berths. Coastal marinas are shown by default; inland marinas can be selected separately.

Does a published price mean a berth is available?

No. Published waiting lists are flagged. Unconfirmed availability means you should contact the marina before making plans.

Can I compare prices in Ireland?

Yes. Select the Republic of Ireland to compare euro tariffs. Northern Ireland prices are shown in pounds. The guide keeps GBP and EUR separate.
